# Queer Data Vocabulary from Podcast Transcriptions

This repository contains Jupyter notebooks for analysing podcast transcriptions and extracting queer-related vocabulary in order to study data and technology gaps affecting sexually and gender-dissident identities.

## The notebooks support tasks such as:

* text cleaning and normalisation;
* stop-word filtering;
* vocabulary and term-frequency extraction;
* co-occurrence analysis;
* generation of frequency tables;
* construction of concept graphs.

The goal is to explore how queer identities, experiences, relationships, practices, and forms of self-description are represented in discourse, and to identify vocabulary that may be absent, underrepresented, or poorly represented in datasets and digital technologies.

The analysis is exploratory and does not aim to define a universal queer vocabulary. Terms must be interpreted according to their social, geographical, linguistic, and community context.

## Research perspective

This work contributes to the study of queer data gaps: the ways in which sexually and gender-dissident identities, vocabularies, experiences, and knowledge may remain invisible or inadequately represented in datasets, NLP resources, knowledge graphs, algorithms, and AI systems.

Computational results should always be complemented by contextual and qualitative interpretation.
